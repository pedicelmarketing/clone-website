How we work

Four habits that decide what a week actually looks like.

01

Assess before prescribing. The first session is spent watching, not fixing. A programme written before anyone has hit a ball is a package with a name on it.

02

Two or three things, not ten. A week is long enough to change a small number of habits properly and too short to change many badly.

03

Match play is training. Practice matches are organised against suitable opposition, because a technique that only survives a feeding basket has not been learned.

04

Leave something behind. With clubs that means teaching the coaches; with players it means going home knowing what to practise without us.
