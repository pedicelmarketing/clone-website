Experiences in Spain

Come to Spainand train properly.

Personalised tennis and padel programmes for international players, groups and clubs, built around level, objectives and how long you have.

01 / Formats

Four shapes a week can take

Each is a starting point rather than a package — the balance of court time, match play and physical work moves with the player.

01 / 04

Individual intensive: one player, one coach, the whole week spent on the two or three things actually holding the game back.

02 / 04

Small group: two to four players of a similar level, sharing court time and playing each other rather than a feeding basket.

03 / 04

Club trip: a visiting group with its own coach, where we run the training and the logistics and their coach keeps the relationship.

04 / 04

Marbella camp: a group based here for a block of days, with daily training, organised match play and the week managed end to end.

All four run for tennis, padel, or both.
