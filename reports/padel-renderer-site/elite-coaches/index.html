How nine days runs

The proportions move with the brief, but the sequence rarely does.

01

Agree the objective. Whether the club wants stronger players, stronger coaches, or a programme it can keep running after we leave.

02

Coach on court. Sessions for players of all levels, plus technical, tactical and physical clinics.

03

Teach the coaches. Education and mentoring for the club's own staff, which is the part that outlasts the visit.

04

Show the club. Masterclasses, exhibition matches and high-performance sessions the membership can watch.

05

Leave a method. Evaluation of methodology and performance, and consultancy on what the club runs next.

Send us your club's objectives for the season.
