## Font license notes (carried over verbatim from brand-brief)

Outfit is SIL OFL 1.1 (Google Fonts). Inter is SIL OFL 1.1. Both are open licence and safe to vendor — no substitution required. The client's brand board sets its wordmark in a wide-letterspaced geometric sans; Outfit is the closest open-licence match with a tall enough x-height to survive both an 80px heading and an 11px label at 0.24em tracking, and its circular counters rhyme with the circular ball monogram. No monospace face is loaded: small uppercase labels are set in the display face via a tracking utility, which is one fewer webfont per page.

Review these notes before vendoring or redistributing any font files. Paid or
restricted fonts remain external until the project has the required license.
