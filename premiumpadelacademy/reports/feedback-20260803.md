## Image upscaling

**Pipeline.** Comfy Cloud, three jobs:

```
LoadImage -> UpscaleModelLoader -> ImageUpscaleWithModel (4x)
          -> ImageScale (lanczos, down to exactly 2x) -> SaveImage
```

Running a 4× model and resampling down to 2× is deliberate: it cleans the JPEG
artifacts in the Wix-sourced originals instead of magnifying them.

| Model | Applied to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `4x-UltraSharp.safetensors` | 10 scene/action photos | Best edge recovery on foliage, fencing, court lines |
| `4x-ClearRealityV1.pth` | `coach-riki`, `coach-juampi` | UltraSharp over-sharpens skin; ClearReality holds faces without going plastic |

| prompt_id | Contents |
|---|---|
| `27a336eb-5ed7-4808-9e49-d0d1c926805d` | hero-home, hero-camps, camps-band, camps-method |
| `ffc6e86c-ce69-4e4c-9592-493aa4afec2d` | club-1/2/3, elite, expect, expect-2 |
| `a7ca9980-2757-42cd-821b-ba23fad60d09` | coach-riki, coach-juampi |

**Verification (HANDOFF §8 trap 4).** `tools/upscale_ingest.py` refuses to install
anything that is not strictly larger than the original and not perceptually the
same photograph. Every asset passed. Originals are preserved in
`upscale/originals/`.

> **Gate note.** The first verification pass used an *average* hash and rejected
> `coach-riki` at distance 132. It was a false positive: that photo is mostly one
> flat green backdrop sitting at the frame mean, so a 4-level luminance shift
> flipped thousands of near-threshold pixels. Visual comparison confirmed an
> identical photograph. The hash was changed to a *difference* hash, which
> compares neighbouring pixels and is invariant to global tone shifts. Rerun
> distance: 79. This is recorded because the same trap will catch the next
> flat-background portrait.

**Duplicate found.** `club-2.jpg` and `elite-inset.jpg` are byte-identical
(md5 `7fec65ab…`). The same photograph is published twice — as the *Más de 15
Años* inset on the home page and as the *Consultoría* card on clubs. One upscale
serves both. Worth replacing one of them with a distinct image.

## Page weight — why the masters are not what ships

The 2× masters total ~16 MB, which would have made the home page ~8 MB. Shipping
that would trade one client complaint for a worse one.

Rendered widths were **measured** in a real browser across all routes at
390/768/1440 (`reports/image-render-widths.json`) rather than guessed. Outside
the three full-bleed heroes, nothing exceeds 1382 px even at 2× retina, so
`tools/responsive_images.py` emits only the ladder rungs measurement justifies
and writes a `sizes` attribute derived from the same numbers.

| | Home | Clubs | Camps | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile 390 @1× | 0.39 MB | 0.48 MB | 0.38 MB | 0.16 MB |
| Desktop 1440 @2× | 2.33 MB | 2.20 MB | 1.88 MB | 0.16 MB |

Masters stay on disk as the quality source; pages serve derivatives from
`assets/r/`.
