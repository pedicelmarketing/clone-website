How we coach

Programmes are what you book. Coaching is what you actually receive. Our academy offers professional, premium coaching designed specifically for demanding players and for clubs that want excellence — personalised programmes built to bring individual and collective talent up to the standard of high-level competition.

High-impact sessions

High-impact individual and group sessions. One coach and a plan that carries over from one session to the next, rather than starting again each time — for players who live in Marbella and train with us every week, and for players here for a few days.

Technical & tactical

Advanced technical and tactical development. We work the technical pillar to refine every shot and the tactical pillar to master reading the game and decision-making — because a shot practised without a reason to play it tends not to survive a match.

Physical preparation

Racket-specific physical preparation. Padel asks particular things of the body — repeated changes of direction, overhead loading and a great deal of stopping — so the physical pillar is there to make sure the body answers the demands of international competition.

Racket-specific conditioning

Match play with analysis

Match lessons with real analysis. Match play is where the work either holds up or it does not. We play, then we go back through it properly: what worked, what did not, and what to carry into the next session.
